Annual Company Newsletter

Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. Our chairman opened the meeting by thanking every
fireman and policeman who helped during the flood. The stewardess on the relief flight
said the effort showed what mankind can do together.

The salesman and the businessman discussed how manpower and man-made delays affected
the project. Each spokesman agreed that the foreman should confirm the schedule, and
that a CHAIRMAN should chair the next session. Every employee should tell his or her
supervisor if hours change.
