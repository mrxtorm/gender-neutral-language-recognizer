Community Bulletin

Boys and girls, welcome to the town fair! The mailman and the waitress from the corner cafe
set up the stalls with help from a local craftsman. A congressman will give a short speech,
and the headmaster of the school will thank every workman who built the stage.

Reminder: each volunteer should bring his or her own chair. The layman's guide to the schedule
is posted on the notice board. A policeman will direct traffic, and a HE OR SHE who arrives
late should ask the staff. The chairmanship of the fair committee is still open.
